# International Shipping Expansion – 5-Year Financial Guesstimate Model

## Project Overview

This project involved building a **5-year financial guesstimate model** in Google Sheets to evaluate an international shipping expansion plan.

The model converts operational assumptions such as fleet size, vessel capacity, utilization, shipment mix, pricing, fuel costs, and other expenses into financial projections for **2025–2029**.

The objective was to understand potential shipment volumes, revenue growth, operating costs, and profitability under the defined business assumptions.

## Objectives

* Estimate annual shipping capacity and container throughput.
* Model revenue across different service segments.
* Estimate operational costs.
* Analyze projected profitability.
* Track financial KPIs over a 5-year period.
* Perform scenario-based analysis.
* Build an executive-friendly financial dashboard.

## Model Workflow

```text
Business Assumptions
        ↓
Fleet & Capacity Model
        ↓
Container Throughput
        ↓
Cargo Segmentation
        ↓
Pricing & Revenue Model
        ↓
Cost Estimation
        ↓
Profitability Analysis
        ↓
5-Year Financial Dashboard
```

## Operational Modeling

The model considers factors such as:

* Fleet size.
* Vessel capacity.
* Utilization rate.
* Number of trips.
* Annual container throughput.
* Fleet expansion assumptions.

## Cargo Segmentation

Shipping volumes were categorized into:

* Dry Cargo
* Refrigerated Cargo
* Liquid/Bulk Cargo

This helped analyze the expected service mix and contribution of different cargo categories.

## Revenue Modeling

Customers were segmented into three service tiers:

* Economy
* Standard
* Premium

Pricing assumptions were used to estimate revenue contribution from each service category and model changes in freight rates over the planning period.

## Financial KPIs

The model tracks:

* Revenue
* Operating Costs
* Net Profit
* Profit Margin
* Year-over-Year Growth
* Shipment Volume

## Scenario Analysis

The model allows business assumptions such as fuel costs, customs expenses, pricing, and operational efficiency to be adjusted to understand their impact on projected financial performance.

## Dashboard

An interactive Google Sheets dashboard was created to present:

* Shipment trends.
* Revenue growth.
* Cost trends.
* Profitability.
* Service mix.
* Key financial KPIs.

## Technologies Used

* Google Sheets

## Outcome

The project converted operational and business assumptions into a structured 5-year financial model. It provided a framework for analyzing shipment growth, revenue, costs, and profitability while allowing assumptions to be modified for different business scenarios.
